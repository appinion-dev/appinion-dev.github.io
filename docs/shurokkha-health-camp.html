1. Introduction

1.1

This Privacy Policy (the “Policy”) is issued and maintained by Appinion BD Limited (“Appinion”, “we”, “us” or “our”), the developer and operator of the Shurokkha Health Camp App (the “App”), on behalf of Sajida Foundation and its Digital Health Programme. It explains how Personal Data, including Health Data, is collected, used, stored, shared, protected and deleted when the App is used to deliver health camp services in Bangladesh.

1.2

The App is an operational tool. It is used mainly by authorised field employees of Sajida Foundation and of Authorised Programmes to register Patients and their family members at health camps, record Health Camp Visits, and capture vital signs and medical documents. The App is not intended for use by members of the public.

1.3

We and Sajida Foundation are committed to processing Personal Data lawfully, fairly and transparently, and in accordance with the laws of the People’s Republic of Bangladesh, including the laws relating to personal data protection, information and communication technology, and medical confidentiality, as amended from time to time.

1.4

By using the App, or by voluntarily attending a health camp and providing information for registration, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy or that its contents have been explained to you.

2. Definitions

2.1

In this Policy, unless the context otherwise requires:

  • (a)“Appinion” means Appinion BD Limited, a company incorporated in Bangladesh, which develops, hosts, operates and maintains the App and maintains this Policy on behalf of Sajida Foundation.
  • (b)“App” means the Shurokkha Health Camp App for Android devices, together with the backend systems and administrative dashboard used to manage health camp records.
  • (c)“Authorised Programme” means any programme, department, project or partner organisation that Sajida Foundation permits or onboards to use the App, acting under the authorisation and instructions of Sajida Foundation.
  • (d)“Authorised User” means a field employee, health camp worker or other person authorised by Sajida Foundation or an Authorised Programme to access the App using Branch credentials issued by Sajida Foundation.
  • (e)“Branch” means a Sajida Foundation branch or any other location designated for health camp operations, organised by area and region.
  • (f)“Health Camp Visit” means the record, identified by a unique visit code, created in the App when an Authorised User starts a visit for a Patient attending a health camp.
  • (g)“Health Data” means Personal Data relating to the physical or mental health of an individual, including vital signs, blood group, prescriptions, medical reports, health indicators calculated from them, and records of health services received.
  • (h)“Healthy” means the Healthy digital health platform operated by Sajida Foundation, including the Healthy patient mobile application.
  • (i)“Patient” means any individual registered through the App, including a main patient and any family member linked to a main patient.
  • (j)“Patient ID” means the unique identification number generated by the system for each Patient.
  • (k)“Personal Data” means any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual, including Health Data.
  • (l)“Processing” means any operation performed on Personal Data, including collection, recording, calculation, storage, retrieval, use, disclosure, correction and deletion, and “process” shall be construed accordingly; and
  • (m)“Sajida Foundation” means Sajida Foundation, including its Digital Health Programme, which owns the health camp programme on whose behalf the App is operated.

3. Scope and Roles

3.1

This Policy applies to Personal Data processed through the App about (a) Patients, and (b) Authorised Users.

3.2

Sajida Foundation owns the health camp programme and determines the purposes for which Personal Data is processed through the App.

3.3

Appinion develops, hosts, operates, secures, supports and maintains the App and maintains this Policy. Appinion processes Personal Data only on behalf of and under the instructions of Sajida Foundation, and shall not use Personal Data for its own purposes.

3.4

Authorised Programmes use the App under the authorisation of Sajida Foundation, are bound by this Policy, and may access only the Personal Data required for their authorised health camp activities.

3.5

Use of the Healthy patient mobile application is governed by the privacy terms applicable to that application. This Policy applies to Healthy only to the extent that data collected through the App is made available in Healthy, as described in Section 7.

4. Personal Data We Collect

4.1

Patient data. We collect the following Personal Data about Patients:

CategoryPersonal Data
Identity and demographic dataFull name, date of birth, gender, Patient ID and, for family members, the relationship to the main patient.
Contact dataAddress, where recorded, and mobile phone number, where provided. The mobile phone number is optional, is recorded once for the main patient, and is used as the sign-in ID for the Patient’s Healthy account.
PhotographA live profile photograph of the main patient, captured with the device camera at registration.
Programme and benefit dataHealthy Card Code, where provided; Sajida Foundation employee status and, for employees, the employee branch and SF PIN; and health camp visit allowance and usage.
Health Camp Visit dataVisit code; visit date and time; the Branch, area and region where the visit took place; visit type (free, paid or Health Card), including any card code used; visit status (pending or completed); and the Branch account that recorded the visit.
Health DataBlood group; vital signs, namely blood pressure, heart rate, blood sugar, blood oxygen level (SpO2), height and weight; images of prescriptions; and other medical documents, such as blood sugar reports, blood test reports and previous prescriptions, each classified by report type.
Calculated health indicatorsValues calculated from the data above, such as age, body mass index (BMI) and screening categories for blood pressure, blood sugar, blood oxygen level and BMI.
Family linkage dataThe links between the Patient IDs of a main patient and the main patient’s family members.
4.2

Authorised User data. We collect and generate the following data about Authorised Users:

  • (a)Branch ID and login credentials issued by Sajida Foundation;
  • (b)records of actions performed in the App, including the records created or updated, the Branch account used, and the date and time of each action; and
  • (c)technical data that may include device model, operating system version, App version, network address and error or crash logs, used for security, troubleshooting and support.
4.3

Data we do not collect. The App does not collect precise device location, contacts, call logs, text messages, or bank account or payment card details. The App does not display advertisements and does not use Personal Data for advertising.

5. How We Collect Personal Data

5.1

We collect Personal Data through the following means:

  • (a)directly from Patients, or from a parent, legal guardian or main patient on behalf of a family member, who provide information verbally to an Authorised User at a health camp;
  • (b)through the device camera or the device’s image selector, when an Authorised User captures a profile photograph or an image of a prescription or medical report;
  • (c)through measurements taken at the health camp, such as blood pressure, heart rate, blood sugar, blood oxygen level, height and weight;
  • (d)through system generation and calculation, such as Patient IDs, visit codes, timestamps, activity logs and the calculated health indicators described in Section 4.1;
  • (e)from existing Healthy records, when an Authorised User searches for a Patient by Patient ID, name, mobile phone number or Healthy Card Code to identify the Patient and avoid duplicate registration. Where the Patient already holds a Healthy account, the health camp record is linked to that account; and
  • (f)from records maintained by Sajida Foundation, such as Branch, area, region and report type settings.

6. Consent

6.1

Participation in health camps is voluntary. A Patient is registered only when the Patient attends a health camp in person and willingly provides information to an Authorised User.

6.2

Before collecting Personal Data, the Authorised User will explain to the Patient, in a language the Patient understands, that the information will be used to register the Patient, record the Health Camp Visit and Health Data, and make the records available in Healthy. Consent is given verbally. By voluntarily attending the health camp and providing information, the Patient consents to the processing of Personal Data, including Health Data, as described in this Policy.

6.3

A Patient may decline to provide any information. Certain information, such as name, date of birth, gender and photograph, is required for registration and for recording a Health Camp Visit. If required information is not provided, we may be unable to provide health camp services through the App. Mobile phone number and Healthy Card Code are optional, and any individual measurement may be declined.

6.4

A main patient may provide Personal Data about a family member only with the family member’s agreement or where the main patient has lawful authority to act for that family member. For a child under eighteen (18) years of age, or a person who is unable to give informed consent, consent must be given by a parent, legal guardian or other person lawfully responsible for that individual.

6.5

A Patient may withdraw consent at any time in accordance with Section 12. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

7. Purposes of Processing

7.1

We process Personal Data for the following purposes:

  • (a)to register Patients and their family members and assign a Patient ID to each individual;
  • (b)to identify existing Patients and prevent duplicate records;
  • (c)to create and manage Health Camp Visits, record vital signs and store medical documents;
  • (d)to support continuity of care by making Health Camp Visit history, vital signs, prescriptions and reports available to doctors and authorised health staff of Healthy;
  • (e)to create or link a Healthy patient account for each Patient, and to display Health Camp history, vital signs, prescriptions and reports in the Healthy patient application;
  • (f)to calculate health indicators, such as BMI and screening categories, that help health staff identify Patients who may need follow-up care;
  • (g)to verify Healthy Card Codes and Sajida Foundation employee status, and to apply and monitor health camp visit allowances and other programme benefits;
  • (h)to manage Branch operations, including daily patient lists, pending document records and Branch patient history;
  • (i)to monitor, evaluate, report on and plan the Digital Health Programme, including through management dashboards that show aggregated figures only;
  • (j)to maintain the security and integrity of the App, keep audit records, prevent misuse and fraud, and provide technical support; and
  • (k)to comply with legal obligations and lawful requests of competent authorities in Bangladesh.
7.2

Healthy patient accounts. A Healthy patient account may be created automatically for each Patient registered through the App, using the Patient’s mobile phone number as the sign-in ID where one is recorded. Records of a family member are linked to the main patient and may be visible through the main patient’s Healthy account. Where no verified mobile phone number is recorded, the Patient may be unable to sign in to Healthy independently until the Patient’s mobile phone number is verified through an approved process.

7.3

Calculated indicators and programme reporting. Calculated health indicators support care and programme monitoring only. No decision about a Patient’s care, eligibility or benefits is made solely by automated processing. Management dashboards and programme reports present counts, percentages and trends, and do not display Patient names, phone numbers, Patient IDs or visit codes.

7.4

We will not process Personal Data for any purpose that is incompatible with the purposes stated in this Section without informing the Patient and, where required by law, obtaining fresh consent. We do not sell, rent or trade Personal Data, and we do not use Personal Data for marketing or advertising profiles.

8. Device Permissions

8.1

The App requests the following device permissions, and uses each only for the purpose stated:

  • (a)Camera: to capture Patient profile photographs and images of prescriptions and medical reports;
  • (b)Selected photos and media: to allow an Authorised User to select an existing image of a prescription or medical report from the device; and
  • (c)Internet and network access: to transmit data securely between the App and our servers.
8.2

Images captured or selected through the App are uploaded to our secure servers for storage against the relevant Patient and Health Camp Visit. Device permissions may be managed through the device settings. If the camera permission is refused, registration and document capture may not be possible.

9. Sharing and Disclosure

9.1

We share Personal Data only as described below and only to the extent necessary:

  • (a)Within Sajida Foundation and Authorised Programmes: with Authorised Users at the relevant Branch, Healthy doctors and authorised health staff, Healthy administrators and programme personnel who require access to perform their duties;
  • (b)Sajida Foundation: with Sajida Foundation and its Digital Health Programme as the owner of the health camp programme;
  • (c)Service providers: with providers of data centre, hosting and communication services, such as SMS services where a mobile phone number is provided, who act on our instructions and are bound by confidentiality obligations;
  • (d)Patients: with the Patient, and with the main patient for linked family members, through the Healthy patient application;
  • (e)Legal and safety: where disclosure is required by law, court order or a lawful request of a competent authority of Bangladesh, or where necessary to protect the vital interests of a Patient or another person, including in a medical emergency; and
  • (f)Aggregated information: with Sajida Foundation management, donors, partners and government health authorities for programme reporting, only in aggregated or de-identified form that does not identify any individual.
9.2

We do not sell, rent or trade Personal Data, and we do not share Personal Data with any third party for advertising or marketing purposes.

10. Data Storage and Location

10.1

Personal Data collected through the App, including photographs and medical document images, is stored on secure servers located in Bangladesh.

10.2

We do not transfer Personal Data outside Bangladesh. Any future transfer outside Bangladesh will be made only in accordance with applicable law, and this Policy will be updated before any such transfer takes place.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

11.1

Health camp records form part of a Patient’s continuing medical record. Patient Personal Data and Health Data are therefore retained without a fixed expiry period to support continuity of care, unless deleted upon a valid request under Section 12.

11.2

Upon receipt and verification of a deletion request, we will delete or permanently anonymise the Patient’s Personal Data without undue delay, and in any event within thirty (30) days of verification, except data that we are required to retain by law or for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. Any data so retained will be restricted and kept only for as long as required.

11.3

Each Patient, including each family member, has an individual record. A deletion request is processed for the individual Patient concerned. Deletion of a main patient’s record does not automatically delete the records of linked family members.

11.4

Aggregated figures in programme reports that were produced before a deletion, and that do not identify any individual, are not changed by the deletion.

11.5

Authorised User credentials are retained while the relevant Branch account remains active. Activity and audit logs are retained for as long as necessary for security, audit and compliance purposes.

11.6

Deleted data may remain in secure backup copies for a limited period until those backups are overwritten in the ordinary course. Such data will not be used or restored for any active purpose.

12. Your Rights

12.1

Subject to applicable law, a Patient has the right to:

  • (a)be informed about how the Patient’s Personal Data is processed;
  • (b)request access to, and a copy of, the Patient’s Personal Data;
  • (c)request correction of inaccurate or incomplete Personal Data;
  • (d)request deletion of the Patient’s Personal Data;
  • (e)withdraw consent to the processing of the Patient’s Personal Data;
  • (f)object to or request restriction of processing, where permitted by law; and
  • (g)lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority of Bangladesh.
12.2

A Patient, or a parent or legal guardian on behalf of a child or a person unable to act, may exercise these rights by contacting the Sajida Foundation Branch where the Patient was registered, at any Sajida Foundation health camp, or through Healthy customer support.

12.3

We will verify the identity of the person requesting to act on it, using the Patient ID, Healthy Card Code, registered mobile phone number or other reasonable information. We will respond within a reasonable time and in accordance with applicable law. No fee is charged for reasonable requests.

12.4

Once a deletion request is completed, the Patient’s Health Camp history, vital signs, prescriptions and reports will no longer be available through the App or Healthy and cannot be recovered.

13. Data Security

13.1

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect Personal Data against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration and disclosure, including:

  • (a)encrypted connections for data transmitted between the App and our servers;
  • (b)Branch-based access controls that limit each Authorised User to the records required for that Branch;
  • (c)login credentials issued, reset and deactivated by Sajida Foundation administrators;
  • (d)permission-based access to the administrative dashboard, with management dashboards limited to aggregated figures;
  • (e)restricted access to servers, databases and stored images, limited to authorised personnel;
  • (f)audit records of data created and updated through the App;
  • (g)confidentiality obligations for Appinion personnel, Sajida Foundation personnel and Authorised Programmes; and
  • (h)periodic review of security measures.
13.2

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. While we take reasonable steps to protect Personal Data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

14. Responsibilities of Authorised Users

14.1

Every Authorised User shall:

  • (a)keep Branch credentials confidential and not disclose them to any person who is not authorised to use the App;
  • (b)use the App only for authorised health camp activities;
  • (c)explain the purpose of data collection to each Patient and obtain the Patient’s verbal consent before registration;
  • (d)collect only the information required by the App, and record measurements accurately as taken;
  • (e)verify a Patient’s identity before retrieving or displaying records, and show records only to the Patient or the Patient’s parent or legal guardian;
  • (f)not take screenshots of, copy, download, print or forward Patient data through messaging applications, personal email, social media or personal storage;
  • (g)not retain Patient photographs or medical document images in personal device galleries;
  • (h)log out of the App and lock the device when it is not in use; and
  • (i)report any lost or stolen device, suspected unauthorised access or other data security incident immediately to the Branch manager or supervisor.
14.2

Any breach of this Section may result in disciplinary action under the policies of Sajida Foundation or the relevant Authorised Programme, and may give rise to liability under applicable law.

15. Children and Persons Unable to Consent

15.1

The App is intended for use only by adult Authorised Users. It is not intended for use by children.

15.2

Personal Data of a child under eighteen (18) years of age, or of a person unable to give informed consent, is collected only as a family member of a main patient and only with the consent of a parent, legal guardian or other person lawfully responsible for that individual. Such data is used only to provide health services and for aggregated programme reporting, and is never used for profiling, marketing or advertising.

16. Data Breach

16.1

We maintain procedures to detect, investigate and contain data security incidents. Where a breach of Personal Data is likely to cause significant harm, we will notify the competent authority and the affected individuals in accordance with applicable law, and take steps to reduce its impact.

Material changes will be communicated to Authorised Users through the App or through Branch communication. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes acknowledgement of the updated Policy.
