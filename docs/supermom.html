Data Deletion and Account Cancellation

At Supermom Technologies Bangladesh Ltd, we respect your right to privacy and provide you with the ability to delete your personal data associated with your account in our Supermom Pregnancy App.

How to Request Data Deletion:

  • Request Deletion through the App: To delete your data, log in to the Supermom Pregnancy App and navigate to the “My Account” page.
  • Initiate the Deletion Process: On the “My Account” page, you will find an option to “Delete My Account.” Selecting this option will initiate the process of deleting your account and associated data.
  • Confirmation of Deletion Request: After you request account deletion, you will receive a confirmation prompt within the app to ensure that the request is intentional.
  • Processing Your Request: Upon confirmation, your request will be processed. We will delete all your personal data from our systems within 30 days of the request.
  • Acknowledgment of Request Completion: You will receive an email notification confirming that your data has been deleted upon completion of the process.
  • Exceptions and Limitations: In certain cases, we may retain some data for specific legal or regulatory purposes. If such a situation arises, we will inform you about the data that cannot be deleted and the reasons for its retention.
