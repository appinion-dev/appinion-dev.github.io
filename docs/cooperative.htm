Privacy Policy for Heifer Collection App

Effective Date: [Insert Date]

The Heifer Collection App is developed and maintained by Appinion BD Limited for use by authorized collection officers of Heifer-supported cooperatives and partner organizations.

This application is intended solely for organizational use and is not available for general public use.

Information We Collect

The application requires authorized user credentials to access its features. The app does not collect personal information from users beyond the information necessary for authentication, access control, and operational use of the system.

Collection data entered into the application is limited to collection amounts and related operational records required for organizational reporting and management purposes.

How Information Is Used

Information recorded through the application is used exclusively to:

  • Record and manage collection information.
  • Maintain operational records.
  • Generate reports and support organizational activities.
  • Ensure authorized access to the system.

Financial Transactions

The Heifer Collection App does not process financial transactions, transfers, payments, or mobile banking activities. The application serves only as a collection-recording and record-management tool.

Data Sharing

Information collected through the application is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes. Data may be accessed by authorized personnel of Heifer-supported cooperatives, partner organizations, and Appinion BD Limited solely for operational, administrative, maintenance, and support purposes.

Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are implemented to protect information stored within the application from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Children's Privacy

This application is intended only for authorized organizational users and is not directed to children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any updates will be reflected within the application or communicated through appropriate organizational channels.
